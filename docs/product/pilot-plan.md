# Pilot safety and operations gates

This is product planning, not legal advice. Get Ethiopian legal, insurance, transport-regulator, and tax advice before operating or collecting money.

Before a real trip, define and approve:

- Driver identity, license, vehicle, insurance, and seat-capacity verification.
- Participant eligibility, consent, privacy notice, retention period, and emergency contact process.
- Named safe/allowed boarding points on the pilot corridor; no road-side improvisation.
- Incident response owner, emergency escalation, support contact, cancellation/refund rules.
- Whether payment is simulated, collected manually, or provider-backed; never mark simulated payment as real.
- An operator dashboard and immutable audit events for all safety-relevant state changes.

Stop/pause the pilot on a serious safety incident, unverified driver/vehicle, payment reconciliation mismatch, or a route that violates the forward-compatible rule.
